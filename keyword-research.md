# Keyword Research: Shipping Container Sales (USA)

Date: 2026-11-26. Source: OpenSEO / DataForSEO, Google US, English. 10 seed searches, 1,189 unique keyword ideas reviewed.
Selection rules: intent transactional or commercial, KD under 25, US monthly volume 100 or more, sales intent only.
Excluded on purpose: rental/hire, tracking, ports and terminals, stock, plans/DIY/ideas (informational), retail "storage bins", brand-name plastic containers, game and cannabis noise.
Volumes are exact-match Google Ads figures and overlap between close variants, so do not add them up.

## Flags (*)
- "shipping containers for sale" (40,500, KD 31) is above the KD 25 cutoff but is the core money term, so it is kept as the hub primary.
- "storage containers for sale" (14,800, KD 12) has mixed intent: it includes plastic bins. Use the steel variants ("storage sea containers", "storage cargo containers", "metal storage containers") as supporting terms and write the page around steel containers.
- "container homes" (60,500, KD 13) and "shipping container homes" (40,500, KD 17) are mostly research traffic. Treat them as hub/guide targets that feed the "for sale" pages.
- Location pages: KD is 0-5 almost everywhere, but volumes are 110-390, so they are quick wins with high CPC ($3-12), not volume plays.
- Mini-priorities: used 20 foot (3,600 / KD 0), 20ft for sale (4,400 / KD 10), buy 40 foot (1,900 / KD 1), prefab container house (5,400 / KD 18).
